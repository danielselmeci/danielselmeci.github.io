The short version

Daniel Selmeci is a tech entrepreneur and award-winning photographer based in United States of America. He founded Polymath Sphere LLC to build creative-technical products, and created Go: TravelApp to make journey planning clearer for people who travel seriously — not casually.

His photography follows the same map: nature, architecture, and portraiture from cities and landscapes worldwide. That work earned four finalist placements in the Shoot The Frame monthly photography awards in 2024, in Wildlife and Landscape. In 2022 his photographs were shown in 14 exhibitions across 11 cities — from New York and Chicago to Berlin, Budapest, Bangkok, and Melbourne. He shoots with a Nikon D780 and DJI Mavic 2 Pro — ground-level detail and aerial perspective in the same practice.

Travel is not a side interest. Time spent in the air and on the ground feeds how Go: TravelApp is designed: tools that respect how exploration actually works, from Rome’s stone to New York’s skyline and everything between.
