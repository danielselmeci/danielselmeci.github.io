The short version

Daniel Selmeci is a tech entrepreneur and award-winning photographer based in United States of America. He founded Polymath Sphere LLC to build creative-technical products, and created Go: TravelApp to make journey planning clearer for people who travel seriously — not casually.

His photography follows the same map: nature, architecture, and portraiture from cities and landscapes worldwide. That work earned a finalist place in Shoot The Wild, an international wild photography award, and a Shoot The Frame feature for Castle in the forest. He shoots with a Nikon D780 and DJI Mavic 2 Pro — ground-level detail and aerial perspective in the same practice.

Travel is not a side interest. Time spent in the air and on the ground feeds how Go: TravelApp is designed: tools that respect how exploration actually works, from Rome’s stone to New York’s skyline and everything between.
